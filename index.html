Before you decide

Questions
schools ask us.

Migration, hosting, backups and support: what to know before rollout.

Ask about your setup
01Can we move our existing Excel or school records?

Yes. We import student, parent, staff and fee records from supported Excel and CSV formats. During the demo we agree which records move across and how they are checked.

02Is it web software or a desktop application?

It is web software, so it works in any modern browser on computers, tablets and phones, with nothing to install on each machine. A mobile app is available for parents and students.

03Is hosting included, and who manages it?

Hosting is quoted separately so you can choose what suits your school. We can manage it for you, or set it up on your own server or cloud account.

04How do backups and data recovery work?

Backup frequency, where backups are kept and who can restore them are written into your agreement before rollout, so responsibilities are clear.

05Who can see a student’s records?

Access is role-based and checked by the server on every request. Parents can only reach their own children’s records, and sensitive changes are logged.

06Can we start on one plan and move up later?

Yes. Your data stays exactly where it is; moving up simply switches on the extra modules, and we adjust the price for the rest of your billing year.

07Who helps our staff after setup?

Initial remote training is included. After go-live, support is provided by phone, WhatsApp and email under your plan’s support terms.

08Are online payments, SMS and WhatsApp included?

The software connects to them, but the providers charge their own fees. We help you set up the accounts and connect them.

09What happens when our student numbers grow?

You pay per active student, so the price follows your size at renewal. Nothing is lost and no re-setup is needed.
